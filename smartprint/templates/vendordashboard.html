Rules & Guidelines

Timely Printing Rules

  • Complete all print jobs within 24 hours of receiving them
  • Update job status immediately when printing starts
  • Mark jobs as completed as soon as printing finishes
  • Notify customers of any delays immediately

Service Management

  • If any service is not available, disable it instantly from Settings
  • Keep your service availability updated in real-time
  • Don't accept jobs for unavailable services
  • Re-enable services only when fully operational

Customer Service

  • Always greet customers with a smile
  • Maintain calm and professional behavior at all times
  • Handle complaints with patience and understanding
  • Never show anger or frustration to customers
  • Provide clear explanations for any issues

Legal & Safety Rules

  • NEVER print licensed books, copyrighted materials, or official documents
  • Do not print any government-issued documents (passports, licenses, etc.)
  • Refuse any suspicious or illegal printing requests
  • Report any inappropriate content to management immediately
  • Only print personal documents, photos, and educational materials

Marketing & Growth

  • Complete jobs quickly to get more orders - Fast completion helps you receive more print requests
  • Build strong relationships with regular customers
  • Offer quality service to encourage repeat business
  • Ask satisfied customers for referrals
  • Maintain clean and professional workspace
  • Keep updated with latest printing technologies

Important Reminders

  • Check your dashboard regularly for new jobs
  • Keep your profile information updated
  • Maintain good communication with the support team
  • Follow all platform policies and guidelines
  • Report any technical issues immediately
