🔧 Favicon Cache Clear Instructions

Step 1: Hard Refresh

Press Ctrl + F5 (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) to force reload the page and clear cache.

Step 2: Clear Browser Cache

Go to your browser settings and clear the cache, or use Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac).

Step 3: Check Favicon

Look at the browser tab - you should now see the new favicon instead of the black "P" icon.

Step 4: If Still Not Working

Try opening the site in an incognito/private window, or restart your browser completely.
